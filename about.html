About MediPriceHub

MediPriceHub is an independent healthcare information and price-information platform designed to help consumers better understand healthcare products, compare publicly available price information and discover legitimate offers.

Our mission

We aim to make healthcare product information easier to understand while encouraging responsible purchasing and professional consultation when appropriate.

What we do not do

  • We do not diagnose medical conditions.
  • We do not prescribe treatment.
  • We do not guarantee medical outcomes.
  • We do not encourage unlawful purchase of regulated medicines.

Editorial standards

Commercial relationships, sponsored content and promotional placements should be clearly identified. Product prices and availability should be dated and verified as far as reasonably possible.
