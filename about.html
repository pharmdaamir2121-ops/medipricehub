About us

About MediPriceHub

MediPriceHub is an independent website that helps people in Pakistan understand medicine prices, compare brands fairly and buy medicines safely. It is run by Muhammad Amir Raza, a pharmacist with a Pharm.D and an MS in Pharmacy.

Why this website exists

Medicine prices can be confusing. The same ingredient is sold under many brand names, in different strengths and pack sizes, and a price on a shelf is hard to judge without knowing what the official maximum is. I started MediPriceHub to help people evaluate the prices of different brands and find what suits them, using official information and plain language.

Who runs it

My name is Muhammad Amir Raza. I hold a Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D) degree and a Master of Science (MS) in Pharmacy. I currently run a medicine distribution business, so I work with medicine pricing, pack sizes and supply every day. That experience is the reason I know how confusing prices can be for ordinary buyers.

Our promise to readers

Official sources first

Prices and rules come from the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP), and each guide lists its sources.

Dates on everything

Every guide shows when it was last reviewed and how to check the current figure.

Fair comparison

We only compare the same ingredient, strength, dosage form and pack size.

No medical advice

MediPriceHub does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor or pharmacist.

What we do not do

MediPriceHub does not sell medicines and does not take orders. Because I also work in medicine distribution, I want to be open about that: this website is an information project, and the guides are written to help readers, not to promote any brand or seller.

Corrections

If you find a price or statement that is out of date or wrong, please tell us through the Contact page. We will check it against official sources and correct it.
