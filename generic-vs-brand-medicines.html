Generic vs Brand Medicines: What's the Difference?

Last reviewed: September 2026

“Brand” and “generic” describe different aspects of a medicine product. Consumers should compare the active ingredient, strength, dosage form, manufacturer and regulatory status rather than assuming that price alone determines quality.

Why can prices differ?

Prices can reflect manufacturing, branding, distribution, packaging, market conditions and other factors.

What should consumers check?

  • Active ingredient and strength
  • Dosage form and pack size
  • Expiry and batch information
  • Authorized sourcing
  • Instructions and storage requirements

When should you ask a professional?

If you are unsure whether a particular product is appropriate for you, ask a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist.
