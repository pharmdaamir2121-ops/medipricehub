Our standards

Editorial Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

MediPriceHub aims to give clear, accurate and honest information about medicine prices and safe buying in Pakistan. This page explains how we choose sources, check and date our guides, handle corrections and keep information separate from advertising.

Who is responsible

MediPriceHub is published by Muhammad Amir Raza, who holds a Pharm.D and an MS in Pharmacy. He is responsible for what appears on the website. You can read more on the About page.

Our sources

  • Official sources come first. Prices and rules come from the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP), including its published notifications (S.R.O.s) and its PharmaPrice Index.
  • Every guide lists its sources. You can see where a figure or statement comes from and check it yourself.
  • News reports are secondary. If we refer to them, we prefer to confirm the facts against an official document.
  • We do not invent prices. If we cannot find a reliable source for a price, we do not publish it.

How we write and check our guides

  • We write in plain language and explain technical terms.
  • We compare medicines only when the active ingredient, strength, dosage form and pack size match.
  • We check figures, dates and names against the source before publishing.
  • We keep health statements general. We do not tell readers which medicine to take or what dose to use.

Dates and updates

Every guide shows when it was last reviewed. Because prices and rules change, we tell readers when a figure comes from an older notification and how to check the current one. When we learn about an important change, we update the guide and its date.

Corrections

If you find a mistake or something out of date, please tell us through the Contact page. We will check it against official sources and correct it as soon as we reasonably can. If a correction changes the meaning of a guide, we update the date at the top of the page.

Independence, advertising and commercial relationships

  • MediPriceHub does not sell medicines or take orders.
  • The owner works in medicine distribution, as explained on the About page. Our guides are written to help readers, not to promote any brand, company or seller.
  • The website may show advertising, including ads served by third parties such as Google. Advertising does not influence what we write, and an ad is never a recommendation by MediPriceHub.
  • If we ever publish sponsored or promotional content, or have a commercial relationship with a company we mention, we will say so clearly on the page.

What our guides are not

Our guides are general educational information. They are not medical advice and do not replace a doctor or pharmacist. Please read our Disclaimer.

Important: Prices and availability can change. Always confirm current details with DRAP's PharmaPrice Index and your pharmacist.
