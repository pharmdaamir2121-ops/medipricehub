How we work

Official sources first

Prices and rules come from the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP), and every guide lists its sources.

Dates on everything

Prices change, so each guide shows when it was reviewed and how to check the current figure.

Fair comparison

We only compare the same ingredient, strength, dosage form and pack size.

Check an official price yourself

The Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan lists registered product prices on its PharmaPrice Index. Always check the date a price took effect.
