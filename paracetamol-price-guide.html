MediPriceHub HomeMedicine DatabasePricesDiscountsHealth GuidesPharmacy Directory Medicine price guide · Last reviewed 12 October 2026 Paracetamol price in Pakistan: official DRAP maximum retail price Short answer: DRAP's last notified maximum retail price (MRP) for Paracetamol 500mg tablets is Rs 534 for a 20 x 10 pack, which is Rs 2.67 per tablet. This is a notification dated 21 February 2023. An MRP is the most a seller may charge. Prices can change after a notification. DRAP later issued an inflation-linked price notification (S.R.O. 595(I)/2023, 19 May 2023), so the figure below may no longer be the current one. Always confirm the current price on DRAP's PharmaPrice Index before you rely on it. See how DRAP sets medicine prices. That means every registered brand of Paracetamol 500mg tablets is covered by the same maximum price. A brand may be sold for less, but it should not be sold for more. How the price changed The price of paracetamol was raised twice in a short period, after the Economic Coordination Committee approved the increases at the request of manufacturers. Here is how the 500mg tablet and the caffeine combination moved between the two DRAP notifications. Product Before (3 Nov 2022) After (21 Feb 2023) Paracetamol 500mg, per tablet Rs 2.35 Rs 2.67 Paracetamol 500mg, 20 x 10's pack Rs 470 Rs 534 Paracetamol + Caffeine, per tablet Rs 2.75 Rs 3.32 Paracetamol + Caffeine, 10 x 10's pack Rs 275 Rs 332 How to compare paracetamol prices correctly Two prices only mean something if the products are the same. Before you compare, check that these match: Active ingredient. Paracetamol on its own is a different product from Paracetamol + Caffeine. Strength. 500mg and 250mg tablets are not comparable. Pack size. A pack of 200 will always cost more than a pack of 100, so compare the price per tablet. Dosage form. Tablets, syrups and drops are priced differently and cannot be compared directly. A worked example Suppose a shop shows a card of 10 tablets of Paracetamol 500mg for Rs 30. Divide 30 by 10 to get Rs 3.00 per tablet. The official maximum is Rs 2.67 per tablet, so that price is above the MRP and worth questioning. Always do the per-tablet sum before deciding whether a price is fair. How to check the current price on DRAP Open the DRAP PharmaPrice Index. Look for the product by name, strength and pack size. The list shows the registration number, manufacturer, pack size, price and the date the price took effect. Compare the "Effective from" date with the date on any price you have seen elsewhere. A newer date means a newer price. If the website is slow or the search does not respond, try again later or on a computer. You can also read DRAP's published notifications on dra.gov.pk. Safety comes before price A lower price is not a reason to choose a medicine on its own. Check these before you buy: The product is registered, and the pack shows the manufacturer's name and the registration number. The pack is sealed, and the printed retail price, batch number and expiry date are clear. The medicine has been stored away from heat and moisture. Many cold, flu and pain products also contain paracetamol. Taking more than one of them together can lead to an overdose. Read the label of every product you use and follow the dose printed on it or given by your doctor or pharmacist. Ask a pharmacist before giving paracetamol to a child, during pregnancy, or if you have liver disease. Important: MediPriceHub provides general information only.
