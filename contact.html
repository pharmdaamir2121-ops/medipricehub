What you can write to us about

Corrections

If a price, date or statement looks wrong or out of date, tell us which page it is on and, if you can, where you found the correct information.

Questions about a guide

If something in one of our guides is unclear, we will do our best to explain it.

Suggestions

Website problems

For example, a broken link or a page that does not display properly.

What we cannot help with

MediPriceHub does not sell medicines, take orders or give medical advice. We cannot diagnose a condition, tell you which medicine to take or change a dose. Please speak to your doctor or pharmacist about your own health. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.

Reporting overcharging

If you believe a medicine is being sold above its maximum retail price, you can also contact the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan through dra.gov.pk or your provincial health department.

When you write to us

Please do not send sensitive personal or medical details by email. We use your email address only to reply to your message. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.
