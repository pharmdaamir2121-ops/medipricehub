How to Read a Medicine Label Correctly

Last reviewed: September 2026

A medicine label contains information that helps identify the product and its proper handling.

Check the basics

  • Product name
  • Active ingredient(s), where listed
  • Strength
  • Dosage form
  • Pack size
  • Manufacturer
  • Batch/lot information
  • Expiry date
  • Storage instructions

Don't guess

If the label is unclear, damaged or inconsistent with what you expected, ask a pharmacist or other qualified professional before using the product.
