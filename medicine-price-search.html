How to read the results

  • Price is the price listed for that pack, in rupees, on the date shown. Prices change, so always look at the "Effective from" date.
  • Per tablet is worked out only for simple pack sizes such as "20 x 10's". For liquids, injections and unusual packs, it shows a dash. See pack sizes explained.
  • Compare like with like: same ingredient, same strength and same form. See brand vs generic medicines.
  • A product with the same price under every brand, such as a notified maximum price, is marked as covering all registered brands.

You can also use our price comparison calculator to compare packs you have seen in a shop.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these prices come from?

They come from DRAP's PharmaPrice Index and from official DRAP price notifications. We copy them and show the date they took effect.

Why can't I find my medicine?

The database does not yet include every registered product. Try the generic name, check the spelling, or look it up on DRAP's PharmaPrice Index.

Is the cheapest brand the best choice?

Not necessarily. Choose a registered, in-date product from a licensed pharmacy, and ask your pharmacist before you switch brands. See 10 questions to ask your pharmacist.
